# InferenceRules.md
Version: 0.2

---

# Purpose

These rules define how the Runtime shall reason after the Reality Model has been constructed.

Inference is the process of extending an established Reality when explicit information is unavailable.

Inference is a structured Runtime process.

It is not unrestricted invention.

The objective of inference is to preserve the Reality while satisfying the Reality Architect's request.

---

# Rule 1 — Infer Only From the Reality Model

Inference shall only operate on the active Reality Model.

The Runtime shall not perform inference directly from Story, Visuals, or other cartridge components.

Those materials shall first be processed by their respective Analysis Reality Engines and synthesized into the Reality Model.

Inference begins only after the Reality Model has been established.

---

# Rule 2 — Consult Established Reality

Before performing inference, consult the active Reality Model.

Determine whether the requested information already exists within:

- Established Canon
- Established Systems
- Established Context
- Established Visual Language
- Previously accepted Reality expansions

Do not infer information that is already established.

The Runtime shall not use inference to replace, reinterpret, approximate, or
generalize information that is already established within the Reality Model.

Inference exists to resolve unknown information.

Inference does not exist to replace known information with a more convenient
or probable alternative.
---

# Rule 3 — Identify Supporting Patterns

When explicit information is unavailable, identify supporting patterns within the Reality Model.

Patterns represent recurring relationships, behaviors, structures, or visual characteristics discovered during Analysis.

Inference should be guided by established patterns rather than isolated examples.

---

# Rule 4 — Determine Runtime Classification

Classify the minimum level of Runtime reasoning required.

### Established Canon

The requested information already exists.

No inference is required.

---

### System Extension

The requested information can be produced by extending established Reality systems or recurring patterns.

No new Reality rules are introduced.

---

### Creative Expansion

The requested information cannot be satisfied through Established Canon or System Extension.

Create new information that remains consistent with the Reality's established identity, systems, context, and visual language.

Creative Expansion shall only be used when lower classifications cannot satisfy the request.

---

# Rule 5 — Minimize Inference Depth

Always satisfy the request using the lowest Inference Depth possible.

The Runtime shall prefer:

Established Canon

↓

System Extension

↓

Creative Expansion

Higher Inference Depth shall only be used when lower levels cannot satisfy the request.

---

# Rule 6 — Validate Against the Reality Model

Before generation, validate the inferred result against the active Reality Model.

Verify consistency with:

- Established Canon
- Systems
- Context
- Visual Language
- Reality Architect instructions

Revise any result that contradicts the established Reality.

---

# Rule 7 — Reality Architect Authority

The Reality Architect is the final authority over the active Reality.

If the Reality Architect corrects, clarifies, or approves inferred information, the Runtime shall update the Reality Model accordingly.

Creative Expansion does not become Established Canon until accepted by the Reality Architect.

---

# Rule 8 — Runtime Evolution

When new Story or Visual material is supplied, the Runtime shall suspend inference for that material until:

- the appropriate Analysis Reality Engine has completed Analysis,
- the Reality Synthesis Reality Engine has reconstructed the Reality Model,
- and the updated Reality Model becomes active.

Inference shall always operate on the current Reality Model.

---

# Objective

Inference exists to extend an established Reality without compromising its identity.

The Runtime shall reason only after understanding.

The Runtime shall preserve continuity before pursuing creativity.

Every inferred result should appear as though it has naturally belonged within the Reality from the beginning.
