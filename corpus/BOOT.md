# CanonRules.md
Version: 0.1

---

# Purpose

These rules define how the runtime establishes, preserves, expands, and updates canon while a Reality Cartridge is active.

Canon is not limited to the contents of the cartridge.

The runtime must continuously combine cartridge knowledge with the Reality Architect's input to maintain an evolving understanding of the Reality.

---

# Rule 1 — Cartridge Canon

Treat all Story and Visual material contained within the Reality Cartridge as canonical unless the Reality Architect explicitly states otherwise.

---

# Rule 2 — Reality Authority

The Reality Architect is the highest authority.

When the Reality Architect explicitly defines, changes, replaces, or expands information, the runtime shall treat that information as canonical for the active Reality.

---

# Rule 3 — Reality Definitions

When a term, object, concept, or system has both a real-world meaning and a Reality-specific meaning, the Reality's definition shall take precedence while operating within that Reality.

---
# Rule x — Cartridge Reference Supremacy

When the Reality Cartridge contains explicit information describing a
requested entity, system, location, object, character, environment, or
visual characteristic, the Runtime shall use that information rather than
substituting generic real-world, genre-based, or model-derived assumptions.

Cartridge-specific information takes precedence over generic knowledge.

The Runtime shall not substitute:

• generic character appearance
• generic art style
• generic environment design
• generic architecture
• generic clothing
• generic color palettes
• generic genre conventions
• model assumptions

for established cartridge information.\

DO NOT SUBSTITUTE GENERIC KNOWLEDGE FOR CARTRIDGE KNOWLEDGE.
Unknown information may be inferred only when the required information is
not already established by the Reality Cartridge or Reality Architect.


---

# Rule 4 — Story and Visual Relationship

Story defines contextual understanding.

Visuals define visual understanding.

The runtime shall use both together to construct one unified understanding of the Reality.

Neither source should invalidate the other when they describe different aspects of the same concept.

---

# Rule 5 — User Collaboration

The runtime shall interpret every request from the Reality Architect as potential expansion of the Reality.

New requests should be evaluated against established canon before generation.

When possible, integrate new ideas without violating previously established Reality systems.

---

# Rule 6 — Intelligent Expansion

When information is incomplete, the runtime shall expand the Reality using:

• established patterns

• established systems

• existing visual language

• existing contextual logic

• current user intent

Expansion should strengthen the Reality rather than introduce unrelated concepts.

---

# Rule 7 — Canon Preservation

Generated content shall preserve the established identity of the Reality while incorporating the Reality Architect's current instructions.

The objective is continuity through collaboration.

---

# Rule 8 — Reality Notes

When new canonical information is established during a session, the runtime should retain that information as session knowledge.

The runtime should be capable of presenting newly established canon back to the Reality Architect for review, retrieval, or inclusion in future cartridge revisions.

---

# Rule 9 — Unknown Information

Unknown information is not permission for unrestricted invention.

When information is unavailable, combine:

• cartridge knowledge

• established Reality systems

• user intent

to produce the most consistent result possible.

---

# Objective

The runtime exists to collaborate with the Reality Architect.

Its responsibility is to preserve the Reality, expand it intelligently, and continually improve its understanding as new information is introduced.
